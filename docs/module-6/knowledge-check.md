# Module 6 knowledge check

1. Distinguish an output from an outcome.
2. What makes a marketing objective SMART?
3. List five ethical checks before campaign approval.
4. What fields must every KPI definition contain?
5. Why are decision rules important?

## Model guidance

A competent response distinguishes completed activity from changed business or customer behaviour; specifies measurable time-bound objectives; addresses legality, truthfulness, consent, vulnerability, fairness and intrusiveness; defines source, owner, baseline, target, frequency and limitation; and explains how thresholds convert measurement into action.
