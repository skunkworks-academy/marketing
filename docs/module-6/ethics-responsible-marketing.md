# Ethics and responsible marketing

Responsible marketing protects customers, the organisation and the wider market. Legal compliance is a minimum standard; ethical judgement may require a higher standard.

## Core principles

- Be truthful and able to substantiate material claims.
- Make conditions, pricing and limitations clear.
- Avoid manipulative urgency, dark patterns and concealed consent.
- Respect privacy, data minimisation and lawful communication preferences.
- Avoid discriminatory targeting or exclusion.
- Protect vulnerable audiences.
- Identify sponsored, affiliate or incentivised content.
- Provide accessible information and reasonable alternatives.

## AI-assisted marketing

AI-assisted work requires human accountability. Validate facts, avoid unauthorised use of personal or copyrighted material, disclose synthetic content where material, and test outputs for bias and harmful inference.

## Ethical decision test

Before approving an activity, ask whether it is lawful, truthful, understandable, proportionate, non-discriminatory and defensible under public scrutiny.
