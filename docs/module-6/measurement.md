# Measurement

Measurement connects marketing activity to decisions. Distinguish inputs, activities, outputs, outcomes and longer-term impact.

## KPI hierarchy

- Business outcome: revenue, margin, adoption, retention or impact.
- Customer behaviour: enquiry, trial, purchase, repeat use or referral.
- Marketing performance: reach, response, conversion or acquisition cost.
- Operational quality: delivery time, data completeness or service response.

Every KPI should have a precise definition, source, owner, baseline, target, reporting frequency, limitations and decision rule.

| Measure | Formula |
|---|---|
| Conversion rate | conversions ÷ eligible opportunities × 100 |
| Cost per acquisition | attributable marketing cost ÷ acquired customers |
| Return on marketing investment | incremental contribution less marketing cost ÷ marketing cost |
| Retention rate | customers retained ÷ customers eligible to remain × 100 |

Customer decisions often involve several touchpoints. Avoid claiming precise causality when the data only shows association.
