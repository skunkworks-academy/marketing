# Practical activity: One-page marketing plan

Create a one-page plan that integrates the evidence developed throughout the course.

## Required sections

- Situation and priority problem.
- Selected target segment.
- Positioning and value proposition.
- SMART objective.
- Product, price, place and promotion decisions.
- Activity schedule with owners and timing.
- Budget assumptions.
- KPI scorecard and decision rules.
- Ethical, legal and operational risks.
- Review cadence.

## PoE artefact

Submit as **Artefact 6: One-page marketing plan and KPI scorecard**.

The plan must be internally consistent, feasible and measurable. Every activity should connect to an audience, intended behaviour, owner, timeframe and KPI.
