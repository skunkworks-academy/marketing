# Objectives and planning

A marketing plan translates evidence and strategic choices into coordinated action.

## Situation summary

Identify the priority customer and market conditions, competitive alternatives, current performance, capability, constraints, risks and assumptions.

## Objectives

Objectives should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound. Distinguish business outcomes from marketing outputs.

Example: Increase qualified enquiries from the selected segment from 20 to 35 per month within six months.

Publishing 20 posts is an output, not an outcome.

## Planning sequence

1. Define the business outcome.
2. Select the audience and desired behaviour.
3. State the positioning and offer.
4. Choose channels and activities.
5. Assign owners, budget and timing.
6. Define KPIs and decision rules.
7. Record risks and assumptions.
8. Establish a review cadence.

A useful plan makes trade-offs visible and identifies what will not be done.
