# References and further study

Learners should use current, credible and decision-relevant sources. Suitable source categories include:

- official statistics and regulator guidance;
- recognised marketing textbooks and peer-reviewed research;
- customer interviews, observations and internal operational data;
- platform documentation for analytics or advertising tools;
- competitor materials, with claims independently checked where material;
- professional codes and privacy or consumer-protection requirements applicable to the learner's market.

## Referencing standard

For each source, record the author or organisation, title, publication date, access date and location. For interviews, record the role, date, consent basis and whether identity has been anonymised.

## Source evaluation

Evaluate authority, methodology, currency, relevance, incentives and limitations. Do not present vendor marketing claims as independent evidence. Distinguish facts, estimates, interpretations and assumptions.

:::warning Current legal requirements
Privacy, advertising, consumer-protection and electronic-communication rules vary by jurisdiction and change over time. Verify current requirements for the intended market before implementing a campaign.
:::
