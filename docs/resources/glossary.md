# Glossary

**Attribution:** The rule used to assign marketing credit to touchpoints or activities.

**Customer value:** The customer's perception of relevant benefits compared with total costs and risks.

**Demand:** A want supported by willingness and ability to act.

**Differentiation:** A relevant and credible basis on which an offer is distinguished from alternatives.

**Market:** A group of actual or potential participants sharing a relevant need and some ability to exchange value.

**Marketing mix:** Coordinated decisions concerning product, price, place and promotion.

**Persona:** An evidence-based representation of a priority target segment used to guide decisions.

**Positioning:** The intended place an offer occupies in the mind of a target audience relative to alternatives.

**Primary research:** New evidence collected directly for the decision under study.

**Segment:** A group within a market that differs meaningfully for marketing decisions.

**Secondary research:** Existing evidence collected previously for another or related purpose.

**Targeting:** Selecting the segment or segments an organisation will prioritise.

**Value proposition:** A concise reason a target customer should prefer an offer over an alternative.
