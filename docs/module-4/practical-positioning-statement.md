# Practical activity: Positioning and messaging

Develop a positioning and messaging pack for the target segment selected in Module 3.

## Deliverables

- Competitor or alternative comparison using at least four decision criteria.
- One perceptual map or equivalent comparative view.
- Internal positioning statement.
- Message hierarchy with core promise, three supporting benefits and proof points.
- Responses to three likely objections.
- One call to action suitable for the intended buying stage.
- Claims register showing the evidence behind material claims.

## PoE artefact

Submit as **Artefact 4: Positioning and messaging pack**.

The positioning must be relevant to the selected segment, differentiated from realistic alternatives and supported by evidence.
