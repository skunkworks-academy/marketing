# Module 4 knowledge check

1. What decisions must a positioning statement capture?
2. What makes differentiation credible?
3. How does a feature differ from a benefit and proof point?
4. Why should message formats vary while positioning remains coherent?
5. Which claims should appear in a claims register?

## Model guidance

A competent response names the target, category, problem, benefit, reasons to believe and alternative; requires evidence and relevance for differentiation; distinguishes what an offer does from why it matters and how it is proven; preserves strategic consistency across channels; and documents material, comparative, numerical, scarcity or risk-sensitive claims.
