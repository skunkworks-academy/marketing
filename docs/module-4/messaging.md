# Messaging

Messaging translates positioning into audience-facing communication. It should connect a customer problem to a credible outcome and a clear next action.

## Message hierarchy

1. Core promise
2. Supporting benefits
3. Reasons to believe
4. Objection responses
5. Call to action

A feature describes what an offer contains or does. A benefit explains why that feature matters. A proof point shows why the claim should be trusted.

Channels may require different formats, but the underlying positioning should remain coherent. Avoid misleading scarcity, unsupported superlatives, hidden conditions and claims that cannot be substantiated. Maintain a claims register for material evidence.
