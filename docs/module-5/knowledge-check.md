# Module 5 knowledge check

1. What are the core, actual and augmented levels of an offer?
2. Which factors should influence pricing?
3. Why must total customer cost be considered?
4. What strategic roles can a distribution channel perform?
5. How should promotion change across the buying journey?

## Model guidance

A competent response distinguishes the outcome, tangible offer and supporting experience; connects pricing to value, cost, competition and strategy; includes switching, implementation and risk costs; recognises reach, trust, advice, fulfilment and support roles; and adapts communication from awareness through retention.
