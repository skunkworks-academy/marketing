# Place and promotion

Place concerns how customers discover, evaluate, purchase, receive and use an offer. Promotion concerns how an organisation communicates value and supports movement through that journey.

## Channel decisions

Evaluate audience reach, convenience, cost, margin, customer-experience control, scalability, partner capability, data access and channel conflict.

## Promotion by buying stage

- Awareness: make the problem and offer discoverable.
- Consideration: explain value, alternatives and proof.
- Decision: reduce risk and clarify action.
- Adoption: support onboarding and early success.
- Retention: reinforce value and identify expansion needs.

Select channels because they reach the target audience in a relevant context, not because they are popular. An integrated plan aligns audience, message, offer, timing, channel, owner, budget and KPI.
