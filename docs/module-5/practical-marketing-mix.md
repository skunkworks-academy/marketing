# Practical activity: Marketing mix recommendation

Develop a coordinated marketing mix for the target segment and positioning selected earlier.

## Deliverables

- Product or service scope: core outcome, actual offer and augmented offer.
- Pricing recommendation and rationale.
- Customer total-cost analysis.
- Distribution and channel recommendation.
- Promotion-channel matrix across the buying journey.
- Budget assumptions and key dependencies.
- Risks, trade-offs and mitigation actions.

## PoE artefact

Submit as **Artefact 5: Integrated marketing mix recommendation**.

The four elements must reinforce the same target audience and positioning. Recommendations must be feasible, transparent and supported by evidence or clearly labelled assumptions.
