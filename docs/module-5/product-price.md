# Product and price

The marketing mix converts strategy into coordinated decisions. Product and price must reflect the target customer, intended positioning and organisational economics.

## Product decisions

A product includes the core outcome, actual offer and supporting experience.

- **Core outcome:** the problem solved or progress enabled.
- **Actual offer:** features, quality, design, packaging and service scope.
- **Augmented offer:** onboarding, support, guarantees, community, financing and after-sales service.

## Pricing foundations

Pricing should consider customer value, costs, competitive alternatives, strategic objectives and channel economics.

Common approaches include cost-plus, competitor-referenced, value-based, subscription, usage-based, tiered, bundled, penetration and premium pricing.

Assess total customer cost, including implementation, switching, training, maintenance, time and risk—not only purchase price.
