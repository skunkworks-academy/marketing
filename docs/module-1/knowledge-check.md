# Module 1 knowledge check

1. Why is marketing broader than promotion?
2. Distinguish a need, want and demand.
3. What non-monetary costs can reduce customer value?
4. Why must doing nothing be treated as a competitive alternative?
5. Write a value proposition using the prescribed structure.

## Model guidance

A competent response explains that marketing covers insight, offer design, audience choice, pricing, distribution, communication, experience and measurement. It distinguishes fundamental problems from preferred solutions and demand backed by willingness and ability to act. It identifies relevant costs such as time, effort, uncertainty and switching risk. It recognises inertia as an alternative. The value proposition must name a target customer, problem, outcome, credible proof and alternative.
