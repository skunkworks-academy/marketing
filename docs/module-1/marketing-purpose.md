# The purpose of marketing

Marketing is the disciplined process of understanding a market, creating value for a chosen audience, communicating that value and enabling an exchange that supports organisational objectives.

## Marketing is broader than promotion

Promotion is only one part of marketing. Effective marketing also influences customer research, offer design, audience choice, pricing, distribution, service experience and measurement.

## Market orientation

A market-oriented organisation gathers customer and competitor insight, shares it across functions and uses it to make coordinated decisions.

## Exchange and value

Customers exchange money, time, attention, data or risk for an expected outcome. Perceived value depends on relevant benefits compared with monetary and non-monetary costs.

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Need | A fundamental problem or desired state |
| Want | A preferred way of satisfying a need |
| Demand | A want supported by willingness and ability to act |
| Offer | The bundle of products, services, experiences and promises presented to a market |
| Value proposition | A reason a chosen audience should prefer the offer |

:::tip Reflection
Identify an organisation that promotes heavily but appears weak at customer research, product design or service delivery. Which marketing responsibilities are being neglected?
:::
