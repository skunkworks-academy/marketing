# Markets, needs and customer value

A market is a group of actual or potential participants who share a relevant need and have some ability to exchange value. Markets can be defined by customer type, problem, geography, industry, behaviour or context of use.

## Understanding customer value

Customer value is the customer's judgement of benefits compared with costs and risks.

**Benefits** may include functional outcomes, convenience, confidence, status, belonging, learning or reduced effort.

**Costs** may include price, time, switching effort, uncertainty, implementation difficulty and reputational risk.

`Perceived value = relevant benefits − total perceived costs and risks`

## Competitive alternatives

Competitors include direct providers, substitutes, internal workarounds and doing nothing. Ask what customers do today, what triggers change, which criteria shape choice, what makes change difficult and what evidence reduces uncertainty.
