# Assessment overview

Competence is determined from three evidence sources:

| Component | Weight | Requirement |
|---|---:|---|
| Final knowledge assessment | 30% | Minimum 70% |
| Integrated practical assessment | 50% | Minimum 70% and all critical criteria met |
| Portfolio of Evidence | 20% | Complete, authentic, relevant and sufficient |

## Overall decision

A learner is declared **Competent** only when:

- the weighted result is at least 70%;
- both summative components meet their minimum threshold;
- all critical rubric criteria are achieved;
- all required portfolio artefacts are accepted;
- authenticity has been verified.

A high average cannot compensate for missing critical evidence.

## Assessment controls

The assessment process should include learner identity verification, version-controlled submissions, plagiarism and source checks, declared AI use, assessor feedback, moderation sampling and a documented appeal route.
