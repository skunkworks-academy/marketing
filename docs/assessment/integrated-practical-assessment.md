# Integrated practical assessment

Develop a concise, evidence-based marketing plan for the organisation, product or service used throughout the course.

## Scenario

Management requires a defensible plan for one priority market opportunity. The proposal must show how customer evidence leads to targeting, positioning, marketing-mix, implementation and measurement decisions.

## Submission requirements

Submit a 10–15 page report plus supporting source files containing:

1. Executive recommendation.
2. Market and customer problem analysis.
3. Research method, evidence and limitations.
4. Segment evaluation and target selection.
5. Persona and customer journey.
6. Value proposition, positioning and message hierarchy.
7. Product, price, place and promotion recommendations.
8. SMART objectives, activities, owners, budget and schedule.
9. KPI scorecard, data sources and decision rules.
10. Ethical, legal, operational and reputational risk controls.
11. Assumption register and validation plan.
12. Reflection on revisions made during the course.

## Professional discussion

The assessor may conduct a 10–15 minute verification discussion. You must explain major decisions, defend evidence, identify limitations and describe your contribution. Failure to demonstrate ownership may trigger additional verification or an authenticity finding.

## Critical requirements

The submission cannot be declared competent when it:

- lacks a defined target segment;
- contains unsupported or materially misleading claims;
- presents research or evidence that cannot be verified;
- proposes unlawful, discriminatory or deceptive activity;
- lacks measurable objectives and KPIs;
- is internally inconsistent across positioning and the marketing mix.
