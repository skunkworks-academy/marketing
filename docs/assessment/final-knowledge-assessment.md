# Final knowledge assessment

Complete this assessment without consulting model answers. The enrolled delivery environment should randomise question order where possible.

## Section A — Concepts

1. Explain why marketing is broader than promotion. **(5)**
2. Distinguish needs, wants and demand using one example. **(5)**
3. Explain customer value using benefits, costs and risks. **(5)**
4. Distinguish primary and secondary research and state one limitation of each. **(6)**
5. Explain what makes a market segment actionable. **(5)**
6. Distinguish targeting from positioning. **(4)**
7. Explain the purpose of a value proposition and positioning statement. **(5)**
8. Explain how product, price, place and promotion should reinforce one another. **(8)**
9. Distinguish marketing outputs, customer outcomes and business outcomes. **(6)**
10. Explain why attribution claims should state uncertainty. **(5)**

## Section B — Applied scenarios

11. A training provider says its audience is “everyone who wants skills.” Identify three problems with this definition and propose a stronger segmentation basis. **(8)**
12. A software firm plans to advertise “guaranteed 40% productivity improvement” without supporting evidence. Identify ethical and marketing risks and propose a defensible alternative. **(8)**
13. A campaign receives 10,000 impressions and 30 qualified enquiries. Explain why impressions alone are insufficient and propose a KPI hierarchy. **(8)**
14. A premium service is priced below all competitors. Analyse possible strategic benefits and risks. **(6)**
15. A company wants to increase paid-media spend despite a conversion rate below its minimum threshold. State the decision that should precede additional spend and justify it. **(6)**

**Total: 100 marks**

## Marking standard

Award marks for accurate terminology, relevant reasoning, application to the scenario and explicit recognition of assumptions or limitations. Unsupported lists do not receive full marks.
