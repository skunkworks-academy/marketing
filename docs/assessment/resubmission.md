# Resubmission and appeals

Learners assessed as **Not Yet Competent** receive criterion-level feedback identifying the evidence gap.

## Resubmission

- One focused resubmission is permitted for unmet criteria unless the delivery agreement states otherwise.
- The learner should revise only the affected evidence and include a change log.
- The assessor must judge the revised evidence against the same standard.
- A substantially new submission or unresolved authenticity concern may require reassessment rather than resubmission.

## Appeals

A learner may appeal when they believe the published process, evidence standard or marking criteria were applied incorrectly. Appeals must identify the disputed decision and provide supporting evidence. A different qualified reviewer should examine the record where practicable.

## Assessment retention

Assessment decisions, feedback, moderation notes and credential records should be retained according to the Academy records policy and applicable privacy requirements.
