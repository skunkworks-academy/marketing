# Learning agreement

Before starting, select one organisation, product or service that you will use throughout the course. A small business, non-profit initiative, internal service, new product concept or realistic case study is suitable.

## Your responsibilities

- Complete your own work and identify any assistance or generative AI used.
- Protect personal, confidential and commercially sensitive information.
- Use lawful and ethical research methods.
- Retain editable source files and research notes.
- Submit evidence in the prescribed structure.
- Respond to assessor verification requests.

## Responsible use of AI

AI may support ideation, editing and structure unless an assessment instruction prohibits it. You remain responsible for accuracy, originality, evidence and decisions. Record the tool, prompt purpose, output used and your validation steps in the PoE activity log.

## Evidence convention

Name files using this pattern:

`MF-[learner]-[artefact-number]-[short-title]-v[version]`

Example: `MF-RMatthee-03-segment-profile-v2.pdf`.
