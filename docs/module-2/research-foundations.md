# Research foundations

Marketing research reduces uncertainty before a decision. Begin with a decision question, not a preferred method.

## Research sequence

1. Define the business decision.
2. State what is already known.
3. Identify evidence gaps.
4. Select proportionate methods.
5. Collect and document evidence.
6. Analyse patterns and contradictions.
7. Convert findings into decisions and remaining assumptions.

Secondary research uses existing reports, public data, competitor sites, reviews, trends and internal records. Primary research generates new evidence through interviews, observation, surveys or experiments.

## Ethics

Use informed consent where appropriate, collect only necessary data, protect identities, avoid deception and comply with applicable privacy and electronic-communications rules.

A useful insight states what was observed, why it matters, which decision it affects, confidence and limitations.
