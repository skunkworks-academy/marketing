# Customer problem analysis

Customer analysis moves from vague demographics to decision-relevant evidence.

## Problem layers

- **Functional:** a task is difficult, slow, expensive or unreliable.
- **Emotional:** the situation causes anxiety, frustration or uncertainty.
- **Social:** the customer wants recognition, trust or belonging.
- **Risk:** the customer fears loss, failure, non-compliance or reputational harm.

## Buying situation

Analyse the trigger, decision process, stakeholders, criteria, barriers and post-purchase expectations. In organisational buying, the user, technical evaluator, budget owner and final approver may be different people.

## Interview technique

Ask about concrete past behaviour rather than hypothetical preference. Useful prompts include:

- Tell me about the last time this problem occurred.
- What did you try first?
- Which alternatives did you consider?
- What delayed or prevented action?
- How did you judge success?

Avoid leading questions and premature product pitching.
