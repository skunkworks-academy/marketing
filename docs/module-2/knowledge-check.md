# Module 2 knowledge check

1. Why should research begin with a decision question?
2. Distinguish secondary and primary research.
3. What makes a qualitative finding useful but not statistically representative?
4. Name four ethical safeguards for customer research.
5. Convert one observation into an insight that affects a decision.

## Model guidance

A competent response links research to uncertainty and decisions, distinguishes existing evidence from newly collected evidence, recognises sample limitations, identifies consent, minimisation, confidentiality and lawful use, and converts evidence into a clear implication with stated confidence and limitations.
