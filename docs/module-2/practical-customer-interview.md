# Practical activity: Customer interview

Conduct one ethical customer or stakeholder interview, or use a documented role-play where direct access is not possible.

## Deliverables

- Research objective and decision question.
- Interview guide with 8–12 open questions.
- Consent note and data-handling approach.
- Interview notes or transcript extract.
- Five findings linked to evidence.
- Three decisions or assumptions affected by the findings.
- Reflection on bias and limitations.

## PoE artefact

Submit as **Artefact 2: Customer research pack**.

:::note Alternative evidence
Where a live interview is not feasible, use at least ten credible customer reviews or documented observations. Explain why the alternative method was used and its limitations.
:::
