# Marketing Fundamentals

This course develops foundational marketing competence through theory, guided practice, assessment and a Portfolio of Evidence (PoE).

## Course profile

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Level | Foundation |
| Delivery | Online, self-paced |
| Estimated effort | 18–24 hours |
| Modules | 6 |
| Formative assessment | Six module knowledge checks and six practical activities |
| Summative assessment | Final knowledge assessment and integrated practical assessment |
| Competence threshold | 70% overall, with every critical criterion achieved |
| Evidence | Verified Portfolio of Evidence |

## Entry requirements

Learners should be able to read business material in English, use a browser and office-productivity software, and access a real or realistic organisation, product, service or campaign scenario.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course, you should be able to:

1. Explain the purpose and scope of marketing.
2. Analyse markets, customer needs and value.
3. Conduct basic market and customer research.
4. Segment a market and justify a target audience.
5. develop a positioning statement and message framework.
6. Apply the product, price, place and promotion framework.
7. Set objectives, select KPIs and build a one-page marketing plan.
8. Apply ethical, lawful and responsible marketing principles.
9. Present authentic evidence of applied competence.

## How to complete the course

Work through the lessons in sequence. Complete each practical task using the same organisation or case where possible. Retain every artefact because the practical outputs become your PoE. Use the formative checks to identify gaps before attempting the final assessment.

:::warning Enrolment and identity
Credential-bearing assessment must be completed through an enrolled learner account. The assessor may request identity verification, source files, revision history or a short professional discussion to verify authenticity.
:::
