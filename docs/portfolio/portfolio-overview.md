# Portfolio of Evidence

The Portfolio of Evidence demonstrates that the learner can apply marketing concepts in a coherent and authentic workplace-style context.

## Required artefacts

1. Customer value and value proposition canvas.
2. Customer research pack.
3. Segmentation, targeting and persona pack.
4. Positioning and messaging pack.
5. Integrated marketing mix recommendation.
6. One-page marketing plan and KPI scorecard.
7. Final integrated assessment report.
8. Learner reflection and authenticity declaration.

## Evidence principles

Evidence must be:

- **Valid:** directly linked to course outcomes.
- **Authentic:** produced or meaningfully directed by the learner.
- **Current:** suitable for the present context and decision.
- **Sufficient:** detailed enough for an assessor to decide competence.
- **Traceable:** supported by sources, versions and an evidence index.

## Submission structure

Use one indexed folder containing final PDFs, editable source files, source notes, the AI-use log where applicable, and an evidence map linking each artefact to the rubric.
