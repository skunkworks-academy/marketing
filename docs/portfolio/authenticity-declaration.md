# Learner authenticity declaration

Include a signed or digitally acknowledged declaration with the Portfolio of Evidence.

> I declare that the submitted evidence represents my own work and decisions except where assistance, collaboration, source material or AI use is explicitly identified. I have not fabricated research, results, customer evidence or workplace activity. I understand that the assessor may request source files, revision history, identity verification or a professional discussion.

## Assistance and AI-use log

Record:

- tool or person providing assistance;
- purpose of the assistance;
- prompt or instruction where relevant;
- output retained in the final work;
- validation and revision performed by the learner;
- affected artefact and version.

Undeclared assistance that materially affects the assessment decision may result in an authenticity investigation.
