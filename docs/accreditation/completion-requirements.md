# Completion requirements

A learner qualifies for course completion only after all requirements below are satisfied.

## Requirements

- Complete all six modules and practical activities.
- Achieve at least 70% in the final knowledge assessment.
- Achieve at least 70% in the integrated practical assessment.
- Meet every critical assessment criterion.
- Submit a complete Portfolio of Evidence.
- Pass authenticity and identity verification.
- Resolve any required resubmission.

## Status outcomes

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| In progress | Learning or assessment requirements remain outstanding |
| Submitted | Evidence received and awaiting assessment |
| Not Yet Competent | One or more requirements have not been met |
| Competent | All assessment and evidence requirements have been met |
| Withheld | Authenticity, identity, misconduct or administrative issue requires resolution |

Attendance, content access or completion of quizzes alone does not constitute competence.
