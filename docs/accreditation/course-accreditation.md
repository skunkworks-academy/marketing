# Accreditation statement

Marketing Fundamentals is a Skunkworks Academy competency-based short course. It is designed for professional development and internal Academy credentialing.

## Current status

This course does **not** claim statutory, occupational, higher-education or professional-body accreditation unless a separate, current approval record is published with the accrediting authority, approval number, scope and validity dates.

Completion may support continuing professional development or workplace evidence where an employer or professional body independently accepts it, but acceptance is not guaranteed by the Academy.

## Quality controls

The course uses:

- published outcomes and assessment criteria;
- integrated theory and practical assessment;
- a Portfolio of Evidence;
- assessor verification and feedback;
- authenticity controls;
- moderation sampling;
- controlled credential issuance and records.

## Future external recognition

Any external accreditation application should map outcomes, notional hours, assessment methods, assessor requirements, moderation, appeals, records and quality assurance to the relevant authority's current rules. Until formal approval is granted, marketing material must describe the course as Academy-issued rather than externally accredited.
