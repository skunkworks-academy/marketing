# Digital credential

Successful learners may receive a Skunkworks Academy certificate and digital credential for **Marketing Fundamentals**.

## Credential metadata

The credential record should include:

- learner identity;
- course title and version;
- issue date;
- competence status;
- evidence and assessment method;
- credential ID and verification URL;
- issuer identity;
- expiry or review date where applicable;
- revocation status.

## Issuance controls

Credentials are issued only after the final competence decision is approved and the learner record is complete. Credential records should be tamper-evident, verifiable and revocable when issued in error or obtained through misconduct.

## What the credential represents

The credential confirms that the learner met the published Skunkworks Academy assessment and portfolio requirements for this course version. It does not represent an academic degree, occupational licence or external professional designation.
