# Targeting and personas

Targeting is the deliberate selection of the segment or segments an organisation will serve. A target should be justified by evidence and organisational capability, not simply by size.

## Targeting approaches

- **Undifferentiated:** one offer for a broad market.
- **Differentiated:** distinct offers or messages for several segments.
- **Concentrated:** focus on one priority niche.
- **Individualised:** adapt at account or person level where lawful and operationally viable.

## Persona discipline

A persona should include context, role, priority outcomes, problems, risks, decision criteria, information sources, objections and proof requirements. Avoid invented details that do not affect marketing decisions.

A defensible targeting recommendation states which segment is prioritised, why it is attractive, why the organisation can serve it credibly, which segments are not prioritised, and what risks remain.
