# Module 3 knowledge check

1. What makes a segment actionable?
2. Why can demographic segmentation be insufficient?
3. Distinguish concentrated and differentiated targeting.
4. Which persona fields should affect marketing decisions?
5. What must a targeting recommendation explain?

## Model guidance

A competent response identifies meaningful difference, reachability, size, evidence and organisational actionability; explains that demographics matter only when they predict needs or behaviour; distinguishes one-niche focus from multi-segment adaptation; limits personas to decision-relevant evidence; and justifies selection, capability, exclusions, risks and assumptions.
