# Segmentation

Segmentation divides a broad market into groups that are meaningfully different for marketing decisions.

## Useful segmentation bases

- Demographic or firmographic
- Geographic
- Psychographic
- Behavioural
- Needs-based

Useful segments should be identifiable, reachable, substantial enough to justify attention, meaningfully different and actionable with available resources.

## Segment attractiveness

Assess problem severity, size, ability and willingness to act, competitive intensity, channel access, organisational fit, lifetime value and legal or reputational risk.

Avoid descriptive segmentation that does not change the offer, message, channel, price or service model.
